🎯 Pick the crop and stage

  • In the calculator choose the “Target mix”: tomato, cucumber, lettuce, herbs… each crop has numbers from scientific references (Sonneveld/Yara, Resh).
  • Choose the stage: “Vegetative” at first and “Fruiting” (or “Mature / harvest” for herbs) later — the ratios change between them.
  • Below the list you see the target in ppm, EC and pH, plus a tip for herbs.
  • Press “Try it now” for a quick step-by-step tour of the calculator.
