⚖️ Charge balance

  • Every real solution is neutral: cations (K, Ca, Mg, NH4) equal anions (nitrate, phosphate, sulphate).
  • If the calculator shows a big difference, the cause is usually an ion the app doesn't track (chloride, urea, sodium, acid) or a wrongly entered fertilizer.
  • The “What causes the difference” card shows each fertilizer's share, and “Improve balance” re-weights doses from “My fertilizers” — calcium first.
  • Adding more of a balanced fertilizer doesn't fix the gap; replacing the culprit does.
