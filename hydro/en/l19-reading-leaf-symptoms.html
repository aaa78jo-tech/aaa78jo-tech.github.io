🍂 Reading leaf symptoms

  • Mobile nutrients (N, P, K, Mg) are moved from old leaves to new ones, so their shortage shows first on the lower, older leaves.
  • Immobile nutrients (Ca, Fe, B, Mn) show shortage on new leaves and growing tips.
  • Yellowing between veins on new leaves = usually iron (check pH). Burnt tips = calcium or high EC.
  • Before adding any fertilizer check pH and EC first — most “deficiencies” are caused by pH.
