🏗️ Types of systems

  • DWC (deep water culture): roots hang in a tank of solution with an air pump — simple, cheap and good for lettuce and herbs.
  • NFT (nutrient film): a thin film of solution flows along channels — excellent for leafy greens and strawberries.
  • Drip on a medium: solution is pumped to bags of perlite or coco — best for tomato, cucumber and pepper.
  • Ebb and flow: the tray floods and drains a few times a day. And aquaponics: fish feed the plants.
