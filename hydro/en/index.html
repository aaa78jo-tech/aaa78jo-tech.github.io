▶ Get the free app on Google Play

What the app does

  • Calculates the grams of each of your fertilizers for any crop — ready-to-use or A/B stock.
  • Compares the result with the target, explains the ion balance and suggests a fix with your fertilizers.
  • Exports the mix as a PDF with amount tables to share.
  • Photo diagnosis of plant problems and a smart advisor for your questions.
  • Greenhouse, nursery and mushroom guides, plus a calendar with reminders.
  • Completely free, in 8 languages.

📊 EC & pH chart for 22 crops

Target values for hydroponic nutrient solutions, from Sonneveld/Yara, Resh and Cornell references. The app calculates the grams of each of your fertilizers to reach them.
