🧫 pH: the key to uptake

  • pH measures how acidic the solution is, from 0 to 14. Plants take up most nutrients well between 5.5 and 6.5.
  • Above 6.5 iron, manganese and phosphorus become hard to absorb, so deficiency shows even though they are in the solution.
  • To lower pH add an acid (phosphoric or nitric); to raise it add a base (potassium hydroxide) — a little at a time, stirring.
  • Measure pH daily, calibrate the meter every two weeks with buffer solutions, and log readings in the pH/EC log.
