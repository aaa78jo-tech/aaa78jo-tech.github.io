🌱 What is hydroponics?

  • Hydroponics is growing without soil: roots live in water that carries every nutrient, or in an inert medium such as perlite, rockwool or coco coir.
  • Benefits: up to 90% less water, faster growth, no weeds, and it fits rooftops and small spaces.
  • Challenges: it needs near-daily checks, and a power cut or a stopped pump is a real risk for the plants.
  • The golden rule: the plant eats only what you give it — so the nutrient solution is the heart of the system, and that is what this app calculates for you.
