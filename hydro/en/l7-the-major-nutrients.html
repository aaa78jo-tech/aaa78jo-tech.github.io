🧬 The major nutrients

  • N nitrogen: leaf and stem growth. P phosphorus: roots and flowering. K potassium: fruit size and taste.
  • Ca calcium: cell walls — a shortage causes blossom-end rot in tomato and tip burn in lettuce.
  • Mg magnesium: the heart of chlorophyll. S sulphur: proteins and flavour.
  • All are measured in ppm (mg per litre), and the app compares your result with the target for each crop.
