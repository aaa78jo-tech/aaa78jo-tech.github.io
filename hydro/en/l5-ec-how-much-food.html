⚡ EC: how much food

  • EC (electrical conductivity) measures dissolved salts — the strength of the solution — in mS/cm.
  • Leafy greens like ⁦1.2–1.8⁩, fruiting crops ⁦2.0–3.0⁩, and young seedlings ⁦0.5–0.8⁩.
  • If EC rises over a few days the plant drinks more water than salts: top up with plain water. If it falls: top up with solution.
  • EC does not tell you which nutrient is missing — only the total. That is why the mix is calculated element by element from the start.
