🪴 Transplanting into the system

  • Transplant when the seedling has ⁦2–4⁩ true leaves and white roots poke out of the bottom of the cube.
  • Start the first week at half-strength solution, then raise it gradually to full strength.
  • Handle roots gently and never let them dry out during the move.
  • Shade newly transplanted plants for a day or two if the sun is strong.
