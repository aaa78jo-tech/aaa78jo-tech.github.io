🍄 Growing mushrooms

  • Mushrooms need neither sunlight nor nutrient solution: they grow on a substrate such as straw or sawdust.
  • The most important step: pasteurise or sterilise the substrate, or competing moulds take over.
  • After colonisation (mycelium spreading) they need high humidity and fresh air to fruit.
  • Warning: never eat a wild mushroom based on a photo or an app.
