🪣 The ×100 stock solution

  • Weighing fractions of a gram every time is tiring, so we prepare two concentrated tanks, A and B, and take a little from each for every irrigation tank.
  • Amount = dose (g/L) × tank volume × concentration. Example: 1.75 × 5 L × 100 = 875 g.
  • At ×100: add 10 ml of A and 10 ml of B per litre of water — one litre of each per 100 L.
  • The “Stock preparation” card in the calculator does all of this, and the export button prints the table.
