📅 Daily care

  • Every day: check pH, EC and solution temperature, and make sure pump and air are running.
  • Every ⁦1–3⁩ weeks: replace the whole solution, because nutrients are used up unevenly.
  • Between cycles: clean the tank and channels to stop algae and bacteria.
  • Log readings in the pH/EC log and put tasks in the “Calendar” so the app reminds you.
