📊 Read the result

  • Expected EC: compare your meter after mixing with this number — a big gap means a weighing or volume mistake.
  • Ammonium share NH4: ⁦5–15⁩% keeps pH stable. Less and pH rises; more and it falls.
  • Charge balance: cations (+) should roughly equal anions (−); a difference under 1 is excellent.
