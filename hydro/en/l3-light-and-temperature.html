☀️ Light and temperature

  • Leafy greens need ⁦12–16⁩ hours of light a day; fruiting crops such as tomato need ⁦14–18⁩ hours and stronger light.
  • Ideal solution temperature is ⁦18–22⁩ °C. Above 26 °C dissolved oxygen drops and root rot becomes likely.
  • Air temperature and humidity together set the vapour pressure deficit (VPD), which drives how well the plant drinks and breathes.
  • Use the app's VPD/DLI calculator to see whether your environment is right.
