🌰 Seeds and seedlings

  • Sow in rockwool or foam cubes soaked in water at pH ⁦5.5–6.0⁩, one or two seeds per cube.
  • Cover the tray until germination to keep humidity at ⁦80–90⁩%, then give light straight away so seedlings don't stretch.
  • After the first true leaf start a weak solution, EC ⁦0.5–0.8⁩.
  • The app's “Nursery” has a 12-crop table and batches with calendar reminders.
