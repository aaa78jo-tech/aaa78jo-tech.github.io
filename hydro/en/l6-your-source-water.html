🚰 Your source water

  • Tap or well water often contains calcium, magnesium, sulphate and bicarbonate.
  • Enter your water analysis in the “Source water” card of the calculator and the app subtracts what the water already brings.
  • High bicarbonate keeps pushing pH up — use the acid calculator to find how much acid you need.
  • Reverse-osmosis (RO) water is almost pure: leave the analysis fields at zero.
