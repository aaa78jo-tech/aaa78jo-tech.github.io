🌿 Aromatic herbs

  • Herbs (thyme, basil, mint, rosemary…) like a weaker solution than fruiting crops, and most like pH ⁦6.0–6.5⁩.
  • Less nitrogen = stronger oils and flavour, which is why the “Mature / harvest” stage has less nitrogen.
  • Harvest regularly by pinching tips, never more than a third of the plant at once.
  • Arugula stores nitrate: dilute the solution a few days before harvest.
