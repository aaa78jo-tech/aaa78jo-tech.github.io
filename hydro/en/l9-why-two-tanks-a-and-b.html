🪣 Why two tanks, A and B?

  • When calcium meets phosphate or sulphate at high concentration it forms a white insoluble deposit, lost to the plant.
  • So fertilizers are split: tank A for calcium (calcium nitrate) and iron, tank B for phosphate, sulphate, potassium and micronutrients.
  • The two only meet after dilution in the irrigation tank, where concentration is low enough to avoid precipitation.
  • The app assigns each fertilizer to tank A or B automatically.
