🔬 The micronutrients

  • Iron, manganese, zinc, copper, boron and molybdenum: tiny amounts (under 3 ppm) but essential.
  • Iron is supplied chelated so it stays dissolved: EDDHA is stable up to pH 9, DTPA up to 7.5 and EDTA up to 6.5.
  • Too many micronutrients turn toxic quickly — never double the dose “just in case”.
  • Ready micronutrient mixes are handy, but check they don't duplicate what your base fertilizer already contains.
