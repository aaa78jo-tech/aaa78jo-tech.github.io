🌡️ Solubility and correct mixing

  • Every fertilizer has a solubility limit: put more than that in the tank and a deposit stays at the bottom. The app shows the amount in red with a warning.
  • The fix: a bigger tank, a lower concentration (×50 instead of ×100), or lukewarm water.
  • Dissolve fertilizers one at a time, stirring until each is gone before adding the next.
  • Keep stock in opaque containers out of the sun, and never mix concentrated A with B.
