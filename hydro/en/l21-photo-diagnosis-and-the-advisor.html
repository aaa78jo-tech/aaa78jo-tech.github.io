📸 Photo diagnosis and the advisor

  • Photograph a single leaf up close in daylight, no flash, plain background — the most accurate picture for diagnosis.
  • The app suggests the likely cause and today's steps, and you can follow up with the smart advisor.
  • Tell the advisor your pH, EC and crop — answers get much more precise.
  • Diagnosis is help, not certainty: if things get worse, isolate the plant and ask a specialist.
