📦 My fertilizers and auto-calc

  • Add the fertilizers you actually own from the “Fertilizer library” to “My fertilizers” — the calculator uses only yours.
  • “Auto-calculate” finds the doses, and “Best mix for my fertilizers” picks the crop that suits what you have and tells you what is missing.
  • In the “Actual result” table: ✓ match (within 12%), ↓ below target, ↑ above it.
  • If a micronutrient falls short the app suggests a fertilizer you can add with one tap.
