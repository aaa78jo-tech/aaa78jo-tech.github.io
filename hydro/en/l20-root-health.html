🦠 Root health

  • Healthy roots are white or cream and firm. Brown, slimy, smelly roots = rot (usually Pythium).
  • The most common cause: warm solution above ⁦24–26⁩ °C with little oxygen.
  • The fix: cool the solution, add aeration, trim dead roots, clean the system and replace the solution.
  • Prevention: an opaque tank that blocks light and algae, and an air pump running all the time.
