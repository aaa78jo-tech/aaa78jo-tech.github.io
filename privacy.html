MediaFinder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026. Developer: JoTech Apps (aaa78jo@gmail.com).

What the app does

MediaFinder searches public photo and video sources (Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, NASA, Internet Archive, Pixabay, Pexels, Unsplash, YouTube and public Telegram channels you add).

Data we collect

We do not run servers and we do not collect your name, email, contacts or files. There are no accounts.

  • Stored on your device only: your settings, the API keys you enter, Telegram channel names, favorites and recent searches. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Search requests: your search words are sent directly from your phone to the sources you enable, under each source's own privacy policy. API keys are sent only to their own service.
  • Downloads: files you choose to download are saved to your phone's Downloads/MediaFinder folder.
  • Ads: the app shows Google AdMob ads. Google may collect the advertising ID, device and usage data to show and measure ads, and asks for consent where required (EU/UK). See Google's ads policy. You can change consent in Settings › Ad privacy options.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Content and copyright

All media belongs to its owners and is shown under each source's license. Downloads are offered only where the source allows them.
